Method & limitations

Synthetic demo data. Pilot results are not audited.

How are growth and regional shares calculated?

Absolute revenue change = USD 150 million − USD 100 million = USD 50 million. Year-over-year growth = (150 − 100) ÷ 100 × 100 = 50%. Regional shares use the 2025 total: North = 90 ÷ 150 = 60%; South = 60 ÷ 150 = 40%. The regional figures sum to USD 150 million.

What are the limits of this report?

This report uses synthetic demo data, and pilot results are not audited. Both annual revenue values exclude tax. No 2024 regional values are available, so regional year-over-year changes cannot be calculated. No causes of the revenue change are supplied.
