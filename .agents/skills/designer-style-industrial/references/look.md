# The look

1. **Type is the structure.** Headlines very large, tight and heavy; Latin words in them uppercase. Every small label (nav, eyebrows, units, table heads, figure names) in the monospaced face, uppercase, tracked. The stylesheet sets these; do not override them.
2. **Visible compartments.** Products sit in one ruled grid (`.range`: cells share 1px ink lines); sections are divided by a full-width 2px ink rule; tables are ruled. Generous empty space frames the big type; data is packed tight.
3. **Square and flat.** No radius, shadow, gradient, glass or blur anywhere.
4. **One red.** `--p-accent` only for one detail per screen: a label, a rule, a key number. Never a fill behind text blocks.
5. **Technical markers, never invented data.** Before each section head a label in brackets, `<p class="tag">[ 02 / 产品 ]</p>`; model names and real units as markers. Never make up codes, revision numbers or coordinates.
6. **Pictures are plates.** Drawings and renders on white plates with an ink hairline, at full legibility; photographs full-bleed or in a ruled frame. Never filter or halftone the client's pictures.
7. **Motion is mechanical and brief:** one entrance, counters rolling, rules drawing in. No floating, parallax drift or spring wobble.
