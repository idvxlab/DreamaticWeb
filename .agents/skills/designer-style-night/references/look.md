# The look

1. **Photography is the decoration.** The hero is one photograph or scene over the whole first screen (`hero--photo` with `data-tone="dark" data-box="none"`), words straight on its calm part, no scrim; a second full-bleed scene may open one later section. Nothing else decorates: no gradients, glows, particles, cards on cards or icons.
2. **Black, white and the pictures.** No accent colour at all; every hue comes from the photographs. One ground for the whole page: black.
3. **Uppercase display.** Headlines and section openers in the condensed display face, uppercase for Latin, wide tracking, tight leading; small labels and nav in uppercase micro caps. The stylesheet sets these.
4. **Ghost pills.** One action per band: a white 1px outline pill with an uppercase label; never a filled or coloured button.
5. **Drawings are plates.** Drawings, charts and white-ground renders sit on cool-white plates (the kit's frames with this style's `--range-bg` and `--panel-bg`) at full legibility, never on black and never filtered. Data stays at reading size.
