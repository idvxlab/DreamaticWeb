# Make the work carry the identity

Choose the presentation from the work: large sequences for visual projects, diagrams and explanations for technical work, or case studies for complex engagements. Establish a clear author/studio identity without letting an oversized generic introduction hide the projects.

For each substantial project, connect context, actual role, constraints, method and supported result. Preserve collaborators, dates and contribution boundaries; do not imply sole authorship or invent impact statistics. Select media through explicit project association, with captions explaining what the visitor sees.

Vary gallery and detail layouts according to aspect ratio and content; avoid giving unrelated works identical generic cards. Keep a shared type/spacing system and navigation back to the project index. Use native media and meaningful alternatives. Where separate project pages are unsupported, provide indexed project sections or disclosures rather than broken routes.

End with an appropriate provided contact route or clear next step. An edit to one case study should preserve surrounding work, established navigation and identity. Submit the implemented showcase rather than a proposal about how the portfolio could look.
