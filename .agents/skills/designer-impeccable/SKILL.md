# Make the visual world feel authored

Choose the surface's visitor mode from the brief:
- **Persuade:** attention, understanding and a real next action; marketing may earn a bold focal moment.
- **Experience:** the work itself leads; make the visual artifact compelling from the first viewport.
- **Operate:** precision, recognizable controls and task completion carry the craft.
- **Read:** comprehension, reading rhythm and contextual navigation lead.

A corporate product landing page is still Persuade. Do not flatten its ambition into a settings-screen aesthetic. Preserve an incumbent world during refinement; explicit redesign may replace its visual language while retaining product truth and working behavior.

## Find a world, not a costume

Identify the subject's unique mechanism and the audience's actual scene. Consider visual systems from that world: materials, graphic traditions, publications, spatial structures or familiar artifacts. Choose an organizing principle that can govern layout, type, imagery and motion, not merely a decorative label. The predictable category template and its predictable opposite are both weak starting points.

Hold a concise internal direction contract: what is distinctive, how the first viewport expresses it, the composition of the following content, type and color roles, and the authored motion if the surface earns one. Make decisions from supplied context; do not require a launcher, product-context file, separate option-selection page or extra confirmation when the brief already resolves the direction. Use [visual-world decisions](references/visual-world.md) for a new or replacement world.

## Commit in implementation

Compose with the real material and its differing densities. Keep specifications readable, let important media occupy meaningful space, and vary section structures. Preserve the difference between brand expression and user-interface chrome. Use available embedded or local fonts deliberately; express type through shape, scale, spacing and composition rather than pretending a remote family loaded.

One authored moment is stronger than the same entrance repeated on every section. In a showcase brief, make the focal motion visible and specific. Supporting states remain quick and useful. Use masks, light, depth, controlled filters or procedural rendering where they serve the chosen world and the runtime can sustain them; do not reduce all expression to opacity and translation.

## Craft at the seams

Follow [the craft floor](references/craft-floor.md) for readability, spacing, states, copy, browser details and motion continuity. Controls, selection, caret, focus, captions and data alignment belong to the same world. Keep the default content visible, provide a composed reduced-motion path, and preserve useful touch and keyboard behavior.

The brief's pinned aesthetics take precedence over generic anti-template advice. Do not fabricate unsupported business claims or replace real content to fit a design. Finish the current scoped deliverable; optional audit, polishing, asset-production or documentation workflows are not automatic extra agents or calls. Claims about rendering or smoothness require observed evidence from testing.
