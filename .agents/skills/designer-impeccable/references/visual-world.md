# Choose a visual world from the audience's scene

Identify the surface's purpose, the product's distinctive mechanism and the audience's real context. Gather possible organizing systems from its material, graphic traditions, cultural home and familiar artifacts. An operating screen should not become a decorative costume of the tool its user already operates. A marketing scene can borrow more expressively from that world.

## New or replacement world

Compare several materially different directions internally. For each, identify the composition, type voice, palette source and one characteristic behavior. Avoid making every candidate a recolor of the same card layout. A literal reading of a product name is one possible territory, not the only source of originality.

Choose the direction that resonates, can be carried through the whole page and is feasible within the available runtime. Hold a compact contract describing its invariant visual relationships. Implement those relationships in the first viewport and substantive sections rather than merely writing a concept name.

## Established world

During refinement, keep effective identity, content and behavior. Extend its rules through spacing, responsive reflow and state continuity. Redesign requires an explicit scope; when it is granted, retain product truth while replacing the visual language coherently. Do not blend the rejected world halfway into the new one.

## Available material

Use supplied photographs or illustrations when the content needs real imagery. Procedural art can express a concept but should remain recognizable as art. Type, optical alignment, scale and composition can supply character when the runtime lacks an external font. Never present a missing asset, unsupported dependency or generated factual claim as finished work.

Preserve a designed static composition, then layer the authored motion. Missing planning files and command launchers do not require recreating a discovery workflow when a prepared brief already supplies the necessary context.
