# Craft in the rendered result

The direction chooses the world; these details make it feel complete. Apply them within the existing deliverable, without launching a separate audit workflow.

- Keep foregrounds readable against every actual surface and motion state, including secondary text, captions and placeholders. Body contrast and large-text contrast need actual measurement in external verification.
- Group related items tightly and separate different groups generously. Give headings a clear relationship to their content and use intentional section rhythm.
- Use a readable body measure, controlled display size and deliberate tracking. Check real text at each target width.
- Carry authored motion through a focal moment and useful states. Do not put the same entrance on every section. Controlled masks, occlusion, light, shadow and bounded filters are valid materials when the runtime supports them.
- Complete hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error and empty states only where the actual component has those states. Do not invent product functionality to demonstrate them.
- Theme the small seams: text selection, caret, focus indication, underline offset, tabular numerals and captions. Keep focus visible and understandable.
- Controls use the visitor's vocabulary; errors say what happened and how to recover. Factual copy comes from supplied truth.

## Recognize a habitual substitute

Repeated equal-sized cards, a hero assembled from a big statistic and decorative labels, excessive eyebrows, meaningless section numbers and a modal for an ordinary inline task often replace composition rather than express it. Gradient text, glass, grids and halo shadows need an actual role in the chosen world. Their presence is not proof of craft.

Let a pinned brief override general stylistic cautions. Fit the available fonts and media to the intended world without claiming unavailable assets loaded. Preserve exact geometry when it communicates a fact, and identify abstract art as art. A reduced-motion or rendering-failure state still deserves the same care as the fully enhanced scene.
