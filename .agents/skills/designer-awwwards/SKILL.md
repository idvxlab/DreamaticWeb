# Build a site with a point of view

Derive the visual world from the subject's material, mechanism, audience or culture. A portfolio, a product launch and a corporate tool need different degrees of theatre. Respect an explicit brief. Keep supplied facts and imagery truthful; an abstract scene is art direction, not a photograph or engineering model of the product.

Before building, briefly consider three genuinely different concept territories internally. Choose the strongest combination of fit, originality and feasible execution. Hold a compact concept contract: idea, metaphor, signature moment, emotion, motion verb, and the frame a visitor would remember. Carry that decision into the actual composition; naming a metaphor is not implementation.

## Compose before choreographing

Every settled frame should work as a poster. Establish a deliberate palette, one or two typographic voices, an optical alignment rule and a varied section rhythm. Put real content into the composition. Give specifications, narrative, evidence and actions different structures rather than wrapping everything in identical cards. Use a provided embedded font or an intentional local type stack; never declare a font that will not load.

Make one signature moment excellent. It must be visible at the place and time the visitor encounters it, with enough scale and duration to read. A brief fade-up or a small hover lift is supporting feedback, not an immersive hero. Maintain useful navigation and readable content throughout the sequence.

## One coherent motion language

Choose one governing driver—time, scroll progress, pointer or scene state—and map the concept-bearing elements to it. Do not let independent effects fight. Give the scene a meaningful quiet state or restrained idle life when the brief earns it. Primary targets respond in the same motion language, with hover, focus and active states.

Implement the intended result with the available browser runtime. CSS, native animation sequencing, canvas and shaders are valid materials; no particular library is the creative goal. Keep the signature's depth, masking, timing and continuity when translating an implementation. Build a composed reduced-motion version, adapt the signature for touch, and pause work outside the viewport or in hidden tabs.

Use [concept and art direction](references/concept-and-composition.md) when choosing the visual world. Use [signature motion recipes](references/signature-motion.md) for a kinetic, scroll or spatial scene, and [canvas and shader material](references/canvas-and-shaders.md) only when a procedural visual materially serves the concept.

## Craft floor

Keep the first meaningful content frame strong and readable. Avoid fictional loaders. Keep the native cursor usable; no action depends on hover. Coordinate foreground/background colors through every state. Preserve keyboard access, useful focus, mobile composition and visible content when enhancement fails.

Judge the actual delivered effect against the concept: could the same scene belong to any company? Does the signature still work after a second visit or interrupted interaction? Is the mobile alternative intentional? Browser performance, visual scoring and screenshot inspection require actual external evidence; do not invent a score or add an autonomous review workflow.
