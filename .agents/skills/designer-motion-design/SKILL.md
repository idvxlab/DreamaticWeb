# Choreograph intent, narrative and craft

Choose the emotion, the micro-story and the physical character before the implementation. Ask what the visitor follows, what changes and where the sequence resolves. Let the subject's materials influence timing: rigid, elastic, fluid and lightweight elements do not move identically.

For a rich scene, orchestrate **primary** action, **secondary** follow-through and restrained **ambient** life. The primary action owns attention. Ambient motion is roughly a fifth of that visual energy and does not compete with the message. Small controls need proportionate feedback, not a decorative scene around every action.

## Set a motion identity

Choose one personality appropriate to the surface: playful, premium, corporate or energetic. Do not default a motion-led marketing hero to subtle corporate feedback merely because the owner is a company. Establish a signature easing family, quick/standard/slow durations and a coherent entrance language. Match amplitude and overshoot to the material and emotional target.

Use [personality and emotional intent](references/personality.md) when choosing that identity, and [timing and easing](references/timing-and-easing.md) for concrete ranges and directional choices.

## Stage the scene

Lead with the focal element, support it with follow-through and give the composition a stable landing. Counter-motion can establish depth when it is subordinate. Do not make all elements equally active. The one-third heuristics help distribute attention: break long travel into stages and avoid moving most elements at once. Bound a group's total stagger instead of accumulating long waits across a page.

Use position, rotation, scale and opacity intentionally; controlled masks, light or canvas can supply a material the brief needs. A significant state change should not rely only on a barely visible fade. For an immersive presentation, the focal transformation needs perceptible scale and a clear narrative, not only hover feedback.

For a whole page, follow [page narrative](references/page-narrative.md): one real subject changes state across sections, built with native recipes that run in this single document. Use [choreography](references/choreography.md) for multiple elements, [scene and ambient patterns](references/scene-patterns.md) only for supporting ambient life, and [interactive patterns](references/interactive-patterns.md) for response and interruption. Conditional references are not a list to load in full.

## Finish coherently

Preserve velocity or continuity when an interaction reverses; do not queue repeated animations indefinitely. Keep the default composition useful if enhancement fails. Reduce spatial movement for reduced-motion preferences, adapt hover to touch, pause offscreen loops and provide meaningful control of continuous motion. Use the available runtime rather than outputting unresolvable framework components.

The result should express the selected personality and story in actual playback. Source-level animation declarations alone do not prove timing, smoothness or emotional effect; those need external device and visual testing.
