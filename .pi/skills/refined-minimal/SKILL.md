# Restrained, proportion-led composition

Create hierarchy through consistent alignment, deliberate spacing and a small set
of text roles. Let differences in scale, weight and proximity do more work than
repeated borders or ornamental containers. Keep enough contrast between heading,
body and metadata to support scanning without making secondary text faint or tiny.
Use system fonts with careful line lengths and natural wrapping.

Whitespace should separate meaningful groups, not stretch a short message into an
empty screen or hide the next action. Adapt spacing to content density and viewport;
mobile layouts should not inherit excessive desktop gaps. Keep essential parameters,
conditions, navigation and contact details visible or clearly reachable. Minimal
expression is not permission to shorten approved copy or omit evidence.

Give a few strong assets adequate space, preserve their proportions and pair them
with useful captions. Without suitable images, use typographic structure rather
than blank decorative frames. Detail-heavy catalogs can retain compact tables
inside a restrained surrounding layout.

Apply Designer's color-evidence rules. This direction can support rich or dark colors;
it does not prescribe beige, black-and-white, gold accents or reduced opacity.
Reserve emphasis for clear actions and meaningful distinctions. Native interaction
states and focus must remain recognizable even when decoration is subtle.

Apply this direction within Designer revision-scope rules. Use the Designer's normal candidate/HTML and validation contract;
no extra moodboard, model stage or proposal is required.
